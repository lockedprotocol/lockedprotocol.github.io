Privacy Policy

Effective October 29, 2026

Dialed Protocol doesn’t collect your data. Everything you enter stays on your iPhone.

What Dialed stores

Dialed stores what you type into it: compounds and doses, injection sites, dose times, lab results and weigh-ins. This information is saved only on your device, in the app's private storage. If your iPhone backs up to iCloud or your computer, that backup may include Dialed's data, under Apple's own privacy terms.

What Dialed does not do

  • No account or sign-up.
  • No servers. We never receive, see or store your health information.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of data with anyone.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, Dialed schedules notifications locally on your iPhone. They aren't sent through any server.

Purchases

Dialed Pro subscriptions are processed by Apple through the App Store. We don't receive your payment details. Apple tells the app only whether your subscription is active.

Exporting and deleting your data

Pro members can export a CSV file from Settings and choose where to share it. To delete everything, delete a compound in the app (its dose history goes with it) or delete the app from your iPhone, which removes all of Dialed's data.

Not medical advice

Dialed is a personal tracking tool. It doesn't diagnose, treat or recommend doses. Follow your prescriber's instructions.

Children

Dialed is intended for adults 18 and older.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
