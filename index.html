Common questions

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone. Dialed has no account and no servers. See the privacy policy.

How do I cancel Dialed Pro?

Open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Dialed Protocol. Cancel at least 24 hours before your renewal date.

I got a new phone. How do I restore Pro?

In Dialed, open Settings and tap Restore purchases while signed in with the same Apple ID.

I'm not getting reminders.

Check that notifications are on for Dialed in the Settings app, and that "Remind me when it's due" is on for the compound. Reminders are set for each compound's next due time after you log a dose.

Does Dialed tell me what dose to take?

No. Dialed tracks the protocol your prescriber gives you. It doesn't recommend or change doses.
