Common questions

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone. Locked Protocol has no account and no servers. See the privacy policy.

How do I cancel Locked Pro?

Open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Locked Protocol. Cancel at least 24 hours before your renewal date.

I got a new phone. How do I restore Pro?

In Locked Protocol, open Settings and tap Restore purchases while signed in with the same Apple ID.

I'm not getting reminders.

Check that notifications are on for Locked Protocol in the Settings app, and that "Remind me when it's due" is on for the compound. Reminders are set for each compound's next due time after you log a dose.

How does the vial calculator work?

When you add a compound, enter the total amount in the vial (in mg) and the liquid in it (in mL): the water you added to a powder, or the size of a ready-made vial. Locked Protocol divides the dose you entered by that strength and shows the units to draw on a standard U-100 insulin syringe, plus how many doses are left. It is arithmetic on your own numbers, not advice. Always double-check your draw with your prescriber or pharmacist.

Does Locked Protocol tell me what dose to take?

No. Locked Protocol tracks the protocol your prescriber gives you. It doesn't recommend or change doses.
